
# 🤖 Los Angeles Restaurant Market Analysis: Feasibility Study

## 1. Project Objective

Conduct a detailed market research study of the restaurant industry in Los Angeles to support the business plan for a new coffee shop featuring automated service through robot waiters.

The main objective is to identify business occupancy trends, establishment chain patterns, and strategic locations to ensure the business's long-term sustainability beyond the initial impact of technological innovation.

## 2. Results

- **Business Category Mapping:** Identified the proportions of establishments by category, highlighting differences between chain businesses and independent establishments.
- **Capacity Planning:** Determined the ideal number of seats based on the market average for successful coffee shops.
- **Location Intelligence:** Identified the top 10 streets with the highest business density and analyzed streets with unique establishments to support differentiation strategies.
- **Decision Support:** Developed strategic recommendations regarding scalability and brand positioning for presentation to investors.

## 3. Tools & Technologies

- **Programming Language:** Python.
- **Libraries:** Pandas (data processing), Seaborn and Matplotlib (advanced data visualization).
- **Data Storytelling:** Google Slides / PowerPoint (exported to PDF) for executive presentation.
- **Development Environment:** VS Code / Jupyter Notebook.

## 4. Skills Developed

- **Data Visualization with Seaborn:** Applying high-level visualization libraries to create professional-looking charts that are easy for non-technical audiences to interpret.
- **Feature Engineering (Parsing):** Extracting and cleaning street names from full address columns using string manipulation methods.
- **Data Storytelling:** Developing the ability to translate complex statistical analyses into clear, visually engaging presentations aligned with business objectives.
- **Density Analysis:** Analyzing seating capacity distributions to characterize different types of restaurant chains.

## 5. Future Improvements

- **Sentiment Analysis:** Integrating customer review data to understand public acceptance of innovative service concepts.
- **Socioeconomic Correlation:** Combining location data with purchasing power information by postal code (ZIP Code).
- **Customer Flow Simulation:** Developing seating occupancy models to forecast ROI (Return on Investment) based on robot efficiency.
